# D-0009 — 3D-ify the art the player already has

**The ask.** Rusty, on 0.1.0 in his own game (2026-10-27): "I fucking hate the way the bread and redstone dust
look", then "How hard is it to take the beautiful art for these things and just 3D-ify them, you took way too
many liberties and slashed way too much quality/resolution". Asked how Heft should draw items a pack already
has better art for, he chose **"3D-ify everything"**: drop Heft's hand-painted textures; build every item from
its live sprite (the pack's where one exists, else the mod's own). Asked which of 0.1.0's hand shapes to keep:
**none** (2026-10-28).

**What the scan found** (the probe's report crossed with the 128x packs in his instance): 68 of Heft's hand
shapes replaced Pixlli 128x sprites (all 20 dusts and dyes, 23 pottery sherds, 19 music discs, the ender pearl,
eye of ender, snowball, cookie, egg, firework rocket); 3 long-tail items were extruded from vanilla's 16x in
place of Pixlli's 128x (blue and brown eggs, disc fragment); 7 of Fresh Food's models were drawn over (D-0008).
Pixlli has no bread: the bread before Heft was Fresh Food's.

**Decision.**

- `LiveModel` makes a flat item model solid from its own quads' sprites, on the game's block atlas: no repaint,
  no Heft sheet, no atlas of Heft's; a pack's LabPBR `_n`/`_s` maps come with the sprite. Nothing of a pack is
  shipped: the geometry is built on each client from what that client loaded.
  - An item whose class has a relief (D-0010) is **raised** by it; every other flat item is **extruded**: the
    game's own flat model made two sixteenths thick.
- Only a model wholly inside the game's flat slab (z 7.5 to 8.5 sixteenths) is touched; a pack's own 3D model is
  left as the pack draws it (D-0007 stands: a flat model another mod chooses at draw time is made solid then, by
  the same relief).
- D-0008's seven items: in place of the pack's plain 3D model, the item's own flat sprite (`item/<id>`) raised,
  with item/generated's transforms, built lying and stood up by each view's right turn (Set It Down rests a model
  drawn in place of the item's own by its box as built, and a raised sprite as built stood the bread on its edge).
- Plants stay crossed (D-0005), from the live sprite; an item with a class is never crossed (a carrot plants a
  crop but is a root: the booth caught it crossed, 2026-10-28).
- **Removed with this** (0.2.0): the shape files and sheets, Heft's atlas, the long tail's repaint, the art
  generator and its tests, the class canon's geometry (D-0003's classes stay, as `assets/heft/classes.json`, the
  table that picks the relief and writes Set It Down's tags, `devtools/tags.py`).

**Sizing, tried and dropped.** The prototype first drew an item as large as its 0.1.0 shape (Rusty: "Try to
preserve relative sizing"); his next word, "keep the RELATIVE sizes of things from vanilla", undid it: every
model is drawn at its flat original's transforms (D-0010).

**Alternatives rejected.** *Remake bread and dust by hand* (Rusty's first pick, overtaken by his next message:
the problem was the approach, not two shapes). *Step aside where a pack has better art* (keeps D-0001, but he
chose to 3D-ify). *Keep hand shapes where no pack has better art* (the ingot's case); Rusty kept none.
