# D-0011 — What a raised item costs to draw

**The problem.** Rusty saw the showcase stutter (the 4070, Complementary, his packs). Measured with
`-Dheft.perf=60` in a copy of that world (`client/Perf`: a minute of frame times and Heft's quads a frame, then the
game quits): D-0010's reliefs drew one quad a cell of a 32-cell grid, front and back, so a raised item took about
1,000 quads (a spawn egg up to 1,976) where an extruded one takes 29. Facing the gallery the game ran 33.7 frames a
second; the same view with every item extruded, 55.2: the raised geometry cost 11.5 ms a frame (about 0.46 µs a
quad). Facing the class lineup it ran 13.2.

**Decision.**

- **Merged** (`domain/Patches`, JDK only, unit-tested): each side's cells covered by rectangles, each grown a
  column and a row in turn while every grid corner in it stays within `heft.tolerance` (0.1 sixteenth) of the plane
  blend of its four corners; each rectangle drawn as a fan through every corner of a neighbouring rectangle on its
  edge, so the surface has no T-junction and no crack. Every vertex is a grid corner at its own height with its own
  normal, so the shading is the grid's. An overlay layer (a spawn egg's spots) lifts clear of twice the tolerance.
- **Coarser with distance**: three levels of detail, chosen at each draw by the item's distance from the camera
  (the pose's translation as the level renderer hands it over): the 32-cell grid, 16 cells past 6 blocks, 8 past 12
  (`heft.detail`). A dropped item's half-sixteenth cell is about 12/d pixels at 1080p, under 2 past 6 blocks.
- A rim wall takes an opaque texel of its cell, the first layer's that has one: a coarse cell can be clear at its
  middle, and a wall drawn with a clear texel is cut away.

**Measured after** (the same world, positions and settings): the gallery from Rusty's spot 56.7 fps (17.6 ms;
p99 21.0); 3 to 5 blocks off it 45.1 to 73.0; facing the lineup 13.2 to 42.9 (worst frame 102 to 36 ms). In his
instance 454 raised items take 464 quads up close on average, 906 at most. The booth holds it: a raised item takes
at most 600 quads up close on average and 1,200 at most. Probe photos old against new (near and mid): no
difference but the spinning drops.

**Alternatives rejected.** *A coarser grid everywhere* (16 cells for a 16x sprite: the edge's shoulder steps again,
the saw-teeth D-0009's prototype fixed with the 32 grid). *Merging level runs only* (a sphere has none: at tolerance 0
its 540 cells stay 540 quads a side). *A coarse mesh with the outline cut by the texture's alpha* (the front and back part at the edge,
and the gap shows from the side).
