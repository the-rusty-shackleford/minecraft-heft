# D-0005 — Plants, spawn eggs, odds and ends, armour

**The ask.** The handoff's §2.2 and phase 5: plants as "cross-plane or layered-plane models, not
extrusions"; the odds and ends that deserve real models; dyes, sherds, banner patterns, buckets, music
discs, spawn eggs; armour pieces' held models (§4.5).

**Decision.**

- **Plants are drawn crossed, at runtime, by what they are.** A flat block item whose block is a bush or a
  coral (`BushBlock`, `BaseCoralPlantTypeBlock`), but not one that lies flat (a lily pad, pink petals),
  is a `CrossedModel`: its sprite on two planes crossed at 45 degrees, as the placed plant is drawn. Every
  layer keeps its tint; the sprite is the long tail's 64x repaint from its own mod (D-0004). It is held,
  dropped and framed as a block is (the game's `block/block` display). It applies to any mod's flowers
  with no list. In the booth's game: 63 plants. A plant a pack already draws 3D (3D Default's mushrooms)
  stays as the pack draws it.
- **Every spawn egg is one kind**, `shapes/_kind/spawn_egg.json`: an egg of four octagonal tiers in a
  pale shell drawn with tint index 0 (the mob's first colour), five spots standing proud of it with
  tint index 1 (its second), as the game tints the egg sprite's two layers. It applies to every
  `SpawnEggItem`, any mod's (80 in the booth's game). `ShapeLibrary` reads `_kind` shapes apart from
  items'.
- **Classes reused**: the 16 dyes are dust mounds in their own colours; sherds and banner patterns are
  plates; dried kelp a plate.
- **New classes** (`devtools/art/odds.py`): record (a black octagonal disc, its label the sprite's
  saturated colours), bucket (a hollow iron pail on a foot with a handle; what it holds is the
  sprite's saturated colours, or for milk and powder snow its lightest), the empty bucket, orb
  (pearls, slime, snowballs, charges, the heart of the sea), nether star, shulker shell, firework
  rocket, goat horn, totem of undying, saddle.
- **Armour is left as the packs draw it.** In Rusty's instance Armored Legacy already makes the armour
  items 3D (only the turtle helmet is flat, so it is extruded). A hand-made item model would also lose
  the trim overlays the game draws through the armour model's overrides. Set It Down draws armour as
  worn regardless (the handoff's §4.5).
- **The palette selector** keeps one colour if that is all a filter leaves (a water bucket's one blue),
  where it had wanted two.
