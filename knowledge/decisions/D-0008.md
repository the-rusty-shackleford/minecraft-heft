# D-0008 — Drawn over a pack's own

**The ask.** Rusty, on the review close-ups (2026-10-26): the cooked mutton "looks too much of a blob. Seems
like its slices of something but the slices are not distinct enough". It was Fresh Food's model, which D-0007
leaves alone, and Rusty had chosen to keep Fresh Food's 3D. Asked whether Heft should draw it instead: "Option
#1, and it should cover anything that doesn't look like what it's supposed to".

**What was judged.** Every one of Fresh Food's 43 item definitions, its `<id>_3d` model drawn offline from four
views and looked at, then in the probe. Most read as what they are (the chickens, rabbits, fish, apples, carrots,
berries, stews, cake, cookie, pie). These do not:

- **mutton, raw and cooked**: a slab kinked into an L, the blob Rusty saw;
- **cooked pork chop**: a striped olive and tan plank (the raw one reads as meat, and goes with its pair);
- **bread**: a flat grooved board;
- **potato** (a plain box) and **baked potato** (a box split along its top), as a pair.

Heft's own were judged the same way before taking their place, and three of them were not better: its steak
class made a pork chop a beige slab, and its tuber a log. So the mutton is a **leg** (a whole joint, full at the
rump, the bone out to a knuckle), the pork chop a **chop** (a round of meat in a rim of fat, the bone out of
its side; the meat at the darkest of the sprite's kept colours, the fat at the lightest), and the tuber two
rounded lumps run together. The loaf was kept.

**Decision.**

- A shape file can say `"over_packs": true` (`build.py`'s `OVER_PACKS`). For such an item, where another mod
  chose the item's **plain** model at draw time, Heft draws its own: the swap is made before the item renderer
  sets the model's transforms (`HeftClient.beforeDraw`, from the same `ItemRendererMixin` wrap), so the
  transforms are Heft's too.
- **Plain** means every quad of the chosen model is on the item's own sprite (`item/<id>`) or its 3D one
  (`item/<id>_3d`), Fresh Food's naming. Its eating frames, its plated variants (a renamed item) and anything
  else drawn from other sprites are left to the pack. Judged once per model, by identity, each bake.
- D-0007 stands for everything else: Heft never overrides a model another mod chose unless the item is named
  here, by looking.
- The booth checks that every item in the list is Heft's in every view, and logs which were swapped.

**Alternatives rejected.**

- *Patch Fresh Food's models.* Someone else's art, shipped as theirs; Heft makes its own.
- *Replace Fresh Food outright.* Rusty kept it, and most of its models read well.
- *Swap after the transforms, as the extrusion does.* A pack's hand transforms belong to its model's own size
  and origin; Heft's model under them is drawn in the wrong place.
