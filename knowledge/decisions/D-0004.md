# D-0004 — The long tail, extruded at 64x

**The ask.** The handoff's §3.3 Tier B: every item no hand-made shape covers, given thickness at bake
time, so "no 2D sprites left" is true. Sized in phase 0 (a static count over Rusty's instance): about
1,467 sprite layers, 24 Mpx at 128x (about 0.5 GiB of video memory with Iris's two copies) or 6 Mpx at
64x. Rusty, 2026-10-25: "64x" (recommended: the source is 16x art, so 128x adds no detail, and the
friends' graphics cards are unknown).

**Decision.**

- **Geometry is the game's own flat model, thickened.** `ExtrudedModel` takes the baked item model the
  game made from the sprite (`ItemModelGenerator`: a front, a back, an edge along every run of pixels,
  every layer with its tint index) and stretches it from one sixteenth thick to two. Its UVs are
  remapped onto the sprite's repainted sheet on Heft's atlas. Any sprite size, any layer count and any
  silhouette come out as the game draws them, with no mesher of our own and no element budget to break.
  The handoff's 16x16 greedy mesh is not used.
- **Overrides are kept**: a bow's draw, a crossbow's load. The original's overrides choose, and each
  model they choose is extruded in turn (`ExtrudedModel.Overrides`, cached per bake).
- **Drawn as the game draws a flat item**: translucent, through `HeftAtlas.ITEM_TRANSLUCENT`, a copy of
  `item_entity_translucent_cull` on Heft's atlas with mipmaps, so a bottle's glass and ice keep their
  partial alpha.
- **The art is made on the client** (D-0001). `GeneratedPack` is a hidden, always-on built-in pack that
  serves `heft:textures/gen/<namespace>/<path>.png` and its `_n` and `_s`:
  - which sprites: every layer texture of every item model that still resolves to `item/generated` once
    the winning packs' models are read (an override's model too), minus items with a Heft shape and
    items denied in the settings (`TierBSources`);
  - from where: the game's own assets, or the jar of the mod that ships the texture (`OwnSprites`),
    never the merged resources. A texture no mod ships, or one animated by an `.mcmeta`, is left flat;
  - how: `domain.Repaint`. The colour is doubled by Scale2x to 64 across, rounding pixel-art staircases
    without blurring. The mask is scaled exactly, because the model's edges stand on the sprite's own
    pixels. A fine grain of our own goes on top. Transparent texels take their nearest opaque colour.
  - maps: flat `_n` at the face's height, and a dielectric `_s` (the handoff's rule 2: art we did not
    author gets no invented relief).
- Heft's atlas lists the sheets by a `directory` source over `gen`. The pack lists only colour sheets,
  so `_n` and `_s` are never stitched as sprites of their own.
- Settings: `tierB.enabled`, and `tierB.denylist` (item ids, or `namespace:*`).

**Measured, 2026-10-25 (the booth's game: vanilla, Create, galestone, voidsteel, Metals and
Materials).** 470 sprites repainted in about 1.2 s at load; 513 items extruded; 1 left flat
(`sculk_vein`, its texture animated); Heft's atlas 2048x2048. Items another pack makes 3D (3D Default's
potion) stay as that pack draws them.

**Alternatives rejected.**

- *128x for the tier.* Four times the memory for upscaled 16x art.
- *Draw the winning pack's sprite* (the handoff's §3.3 "through ResourceManager"). It draws Pixlli
  (D-0001).
- *A greedy box mesh from a 16x16 mask.* It needs a budget and a fallback, and cannot carry a layer's
  tint the way the game's own quads do.
