# D-0001 — Only our own art is drawn

**The ask.** nfx's handoff (`knowledge/sources/3D-ITEM-REPLACER-HANDOFF.md`) wants every flat item
sprite drawn as a styled 3D model, with 128x art and LabPBR maps in Pixlli's idiom. Its §5.2 derives
each new texture from Pixlli's block art, and its §3.2 maps model faces onto the existing item sprite
(which, on a client running Pixlli, is Pixlli's). Rusty, 2026-10-25: "only make the resolution (128x
where possible, and utilize labpbr texturing techniques where possible). No pixlli textures or assets
should be utilized, all assets and textures created for this mod must be house made."

**Decision.** Rusty chose each of these when asked, the recommended option every time.

- **Numbers may cross; pixels may not** (Survivalist Armor's D-0002, reused).
  - `devtools/art/measure/` runs the shared `minecraft mods/tools/pbr/measure.py` over Rusty's own copy
    of the pack and writes `devtools/art/heft-style.json`: value percentiles, a palette of at most
    eight colours, grain, rim, smoothness, F0, height and tilt per material class.
  - The painter (`devtools/art/paint.py` and everything it imports) never opens a pack.
    `tests/test_wall.py` fails if it names one, imports the measuring side, or finds anything
    image-sized in the numbers.
- **No face is mapped onto a pack's sprite.** Every face of a solid model samples Heft's own sheet.
  On a client with Pixlli, the handoff's UV trick would have drawn Pixlli through our geometry.
- **The long tail (Tier B) is repainted on the client** from each mod's own sprite, read from that
  mod's jar (the vanilla pack for `minecraft:`), never from the merged resource manager. It is
  computed where the files already are, so nothing derived from another author's art is shipped.
- **3D Default's and Refined Tools' ~404 items are left as they draw.** Heft fills the holes;
  replacing them is a later per-category setting.
- **The handoff's §5.1 rules stand**, being technique, not assets: height from the design, never
  from albedo; the crest anchored at 253, a face near 250, a seam near 208, nothing under 175, depth
  capped at 62 levels; maps rotated with their material; transparent texels filled from the faces
  before any filtering; emission only where something glows; LabPBR's metal codes for F0 (230 iron,
  231 gold, 234 copper). `tools/pbr/labpbr.py` encodes and checks these, and `tests/test_textures.py`
  holds every sheet to them.

**Alternatives rejected.**

- *Derive the art from Pixlli's block textures* (the handoff's §5.2). Rusty ruled it out.
- *Map faces onto the winning pack's item sprite* (the handoff's §3.2). It is free, but it draws
  Pixlli.
- *Original art, unmeasured.* It would read as a different pack beside Pixlli's blocks.

**Found while measuring.** Pixlli has quartz: `block/quartz_block_side` (the handoff looked for
`quartz_block` and reported none).
