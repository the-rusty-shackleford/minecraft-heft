# D-0007 — Depth at draw time, whoever chose the model

**What the probe found** (the booth run in a copy of Rusty's own instance: 129 mods, his packs, Pixlli,
Complementary). Heft's baked models were in place (493 solid), but for 45 items another mod chose the drawn
model at draw time:

- **Modefite** reads 1.21.4 item definitions on 1.21.1, so **Fresh Food is alive in this pack** (the
  handoff's §2.2 called it dead), with Refined Tools and Armored Legacy. Where a definition exists,
  Modefite's head injections into `ItemRenderer` pick the model, per view. Fresh Food draws its 3D food
  in the hand and a flat vanilla sprite in the inventory, on the ground and in frames; Refined Tools'
  bow and flint and steel are 3D in first person and flat elsewhere.
- **VanillaBackport** draws spawn eggs with its own code (`SpawnEggRenderer`, quads pushed itself) while
  its client setting `use_legacy_spawn_eggs` is false, never through the item renderer.

**Decision.**

- **Heft never overrides a model another mod or pack chose; it gives depth to whatever would be drawn
  flat.** A MixinExtras `@WrapOperation` on the `ClientHooks.handleCameraTransforms` call inside
  `ItemRenderer.render` (`mixin/client/ItemRendererMixin`) sees the model that is about to be drawn:
  - a flat one (not 3D, not custom), whoever chose it, is drawn as an `ExtrudedModel` of itself (cached
    until the next bake), from its own repainted sprite;
  - Heft's own models and any 3D model pass untouched.
  The swap is made there, after every head injection has decided, not at the head. Modefite re-renders
  a ground item whenever the model it is handed is not its own, by identity, so a swap at the head
  looped until the stack overflowed (seen in the probe). The extruded model keeps the original's
  transforms, so it is drawn exactly where the original would have been.
- **Every flat model's sprites are repainted, shaped items' included.** Fresh Food's flat views draw
  vanilla's own `item/apple`, and `TierBSources` had skipped the sprites of items Heft shapes. 1,370
  sprites in Rusty's instance, 2.6 s at load.
- **What is drawn is recorded per item and view** (`HeftClient.Decision`: HEFT, EXTRUDED, LEFT_3D,
  CUSTOM, FLAT). `/heft inspect` shows it, and the booth checks it: no item Heft covers is drawn flat in
  any view.
- **Spawn eggs under VanillaBackport stay its own flat art.** It draws them by code Heft does not see. A
  player who sets `use_legacy_spawn_eggs = true` gets Heft's tinted egg. Left so, as the principle says.

**Consequence for Fresh Food** (the handoff's §8.3, decided before the probe on the belief it was dead):
in this pack Fresh Food keeps its 3D food in the hand, and Heft gives depth to its flat views. Heft's own
food shapes show only where Fresh Food has no definition (Farmer's Delight, eggs, bowls of its own) and
for anyone without Fresh Food. Whether Heft's food should replace Fresh Food's outright is Rusty's call.

**Measured in Rusty's instance (2026-10-26):** 493 solid, 863 extruded, 88 crossed, 19 flat (Alex's
Mobs, Dusty Decorations, Display Delight and others whose sprites are animated or not square), 3,454 3D
by other packs and mods. The block atlas stays 8192x8192; Heft's atlas 4096x4096. All 17 booth checks
pass. Old Cannons (a Fabric mod through Sinytra Connector) was left out of the copy: the dev launcher
refuses a Fabric jar that the game skips with a warning.
