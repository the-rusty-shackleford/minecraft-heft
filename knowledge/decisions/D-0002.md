# D-0002 — Heft's own atlas, one model for every view

**The ask.** About 300 hand-made sheets and over a thousand repainted ones, each at 128 texels to the
block, must be drawn in every view an item has, and Set It Down must measure and lay them down.

**What forced it.** Rusty's block atlas is 8192x8192 with under 1.73 Mpx free (Survivalist Armor's
D-0008, measured on a copy of his client). Tier A alone (about 6 Mpx) would double it to 16384x8192.

**Decision.**

- **The sheets are stitched on `heft:textures/atlas/items.png`**, listed in
  `assets/heft/atlases/items.json` (written by `devtools/art/build.py`; atlas lists from every pack are
  merged, which is how the booth adds its loud twin). It is registered through NeoForge's
  `RegisterMaterialAtlasesEvent`, not as a `TextureAtlasHolder`:
  - the model manager stitches it with the game's mipmap levels, where a holder is stitched at
    level 0 (`TextureAtlasHolder.reload` passes 0);
  - its sprites are there when models bake, so `ModifyBakingResult` bakes every solid model at once,
    with no lazy bake and no re-bake on restitch (Survivalist Armor's kits bake lazily for want of this).
- **Drawn by `HeftAtlas.ITEM`**: the game's own `rendertype_entity_cutout` shader, as every item
  is, on this atlas with mipmaps on. 128 texels shrink to a 16-pixel slot without shimmering. The
  block atlas's item render types sample without mipmaps.
- **One `SolidModel` for every view**, put in place of the item's own at `ModifyBakingResult`. Its
  `getRenderTypes` names `HeftAtlas.ITEM`, and NeoForge's `ItemRenderer.render` asks every model
  for its render types (read in its source). So the inventory, both hands, the ground, frames, the
  head and Set It Down (whose measuring and drawing go through `renderStatic`) all draw it, with no
  mixin. Its particle icon stays the item's own, on the block atlas, where break particles are drawn.
- Iris loads LabPBR maps for every `TextureAtlas` (Survivalist Armor's D-0008, seen in its booth). For
  this atlas it is checked in Heft's booth by the loud twin (`devtools/booth/loud.py`).

- **Sheets live under `assets/heft/textures/solid/`, never `textures/item/`.** The game's block atlas
  (`minecraft:atlases/blocks.json`) has a `directory` source over `item` in every namespace, so a sheet
  there is stitched onto the block atlas as well. Heft's booth caught this. `tests/test_wall.py` holds
  the path, and the booth checks that no Heft sprite is on the block atlas.

**Alternatives rejected.**

- *JSON item models in a top-priority built-in pack* (the handoff's §3.2). A model file's textures
  resolve on the block atlas only, and that atlas is full.
- *A `TextureAtlasHolder`* (Survivalist Armor's way). It cannot stitch mipmaps, and its sprites
  arrive after models bake.
